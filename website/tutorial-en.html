Tutorial

Eight steps to play, write and keep a rhythm in Batruc. Names in quotes are the ones the interface shows.

1. Start · the seed

Open Batruc and click “Play”. The seed — the eight-character code in the header — writes the whole composition: kick, snare and hi-hat figures, the bass key and the form across seven cycles.

  • “New seed” draws another composition.
  • Type a code in the seed field and press Enter to return to a known seed.
  • “VOICES” chooses how many voices play.

2. RHYTHM or GENERATIVE

The mode button in the header switches between the two ways of playing.

  • “RHYTHM” plays exactly what is written: cells, MIDI, figures. Nothing acts behind your back.
  • “GENERATIVE” adds the field masks, the intensity laws and the form.
  • “GEN”, in the bottom bar, sets the depth of GENERATIVE (30 % to start).

3. Read and edit in the Study

In “Compose”, “STUDY” shows the shared cycle: each voice is a ring, each dot an attack.

  • Click an empty part of a ring to add an attack; right-click or double-click a dot to remove it.
  • Drag a dot to move that attack only; drag an empty part of the ring to shift the whole stem.
  • Shift + drag inwards or outwards sets the strength.
  • “UNDO”, “REDO”, “CLEAR” and “RESTORE” act on the selected voice.

4. Other figures

The Study's “RHYTHM” button opens the writing menu for the selected voice.

  • Euclidean redistribution of the cell's attacks.
  • “Write pendulum figures” or figures “from another model”: billiard, Lissajous, Fourier, rolling curves.
  • “RHYTHMS / MIDI” brings a rhythm from the catalogue (Groove MIDI Dataset) or a MIDI file of your own.

5. The snare

Ornaments are stored on the note: one mark per attack, and the strokes are born at playback.

  • In the Study's “RHYTHM” menu: “Ornament (snare)”, “Snare groove (genre)” — frevo, maracatu, baião — and “Measured microtiming”.
  • In the “Mixer”, each stem's “ORN” button switches between the plain beat and the full snare.

6. The bass and the key

The seed chooses a key — twelve tonics and ten modes — and the bass follows the composition.

  • In the “Bass” tab, “PHRASE” (on by default) gives the bass its own voice: a degree progression, answers and passing notes.
  • The bass staff, 16 beats long, sits above the graph; right-click it to export MusicXML.
  • In the “Mixer”, “DRUMS IN KEY” tunes the percussion to the seed's key (off by default).

7. The drum staff

In the Study, the “♩ STAFF ⇄ ◯ CIRCLE” chip swaps the circle for the drum staff.

  • The staff shows the ornament signs and the sticking (R/L).
  • Right-click: “Export MusicXML (drums)…”, to open it in MuseScore or another score editor.

8. Scenes and recording

Keep versions and record what you played.

  • “Scenes” keeps versions in the session and exports or opens a .batruc.txt file.
  • “REC” records the stereo master as 24-bit WAV; the box next to it limits the length (5 min to start).
  • “Learn” opens LEARN inside the app; “About” shows the version, credits and licence.

Central rule

Generation writes; it does not filter behind your back. What the model writes stays editable, and RHYTHM mode plays exactly what is written.
